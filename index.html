Smart QR Code FAQs

What is a smart QR code?

A smart QR code adapts its design to its destination. Instead of a generic black-and-white code, Smart QR analyzes your link and generates a branded, colorful QR code with the right dot style, logo and frame — while verifying it still scans perfectly.

Are QR codes with logos safe to scan?

They are when designed correctly. A logo covers part of the code, so the error-correction level must be high enough to compensate. Smart QR measures logo coverage and contrast automatically and repairs risky designs, so every download scans reliably.

Is Smart QR really free?

Yes. Generating, customizing and downloading QR codes — including high-resolution 1600px PNG and vector SVG exports — is completely free with no account required.

Do my links leave my browser?

No. Smart QR runs entirely client-side. Your URLs are never sent to a server, stored or tracked.

Can I put my logo on a QR code for free?

Yes. Upload any logo or pick a platform logo (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and more), and Smart QR places it in the center while measuring logo coverage and error correction — then auto-repairs the design if scanability is at risk. All free.

How do I make a QR code for my restaurant menu or business?

Paste your menu or website link, click Smart Design for an on-brand look (or choose a preset like Dining Menu or Corporate), then download a print-ready PNG or SVG. No account, no watermark, free forever.
